A LAW TO MAKE BETTER PROVISION FOR THE
RATE OF INTEREST ON DEBTS AND OBLIGATIONS,

‘16th November, 1944.)
1. This Law may be cited as the Interest Law.

2. The rate of interest on any debt or obligation
contracted after the 16th day of November, 1944, shall
not exceed nine per centum per annum and no interest
at a greater rate shall be recovered on any such debt or
obligation,

3. (1) The amount which may be recovered by action
as arrears of interest on any debt or obligation shall not
exceed the amount of the principal debt or obligation
in respect of which such interest is payable.

(2) For the purposes of this section, any proceedings
taken under the provisions of the Sale of Mortgaged
Property Law, or any Law amending or substituted for
the same, shall be deemed to be an action.
