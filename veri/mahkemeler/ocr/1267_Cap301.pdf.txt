To PROVIDE FOR THE REGULATION OF WIRELESS TELE-
GRAPHY ON FOREIGN WARSHIPS WITHIN THY” TERRI-
TORIAL WATERS OF CYPRUS.

[20th November, 1925.]
=

1. This Law may be cited as the Foreign Warships (Wire-
less Telegraphy) Law.

2. In this Law—

“foreign warships '' mean any men-of-war, other
than British, and includes service aircraft accom-
panying them;

“wireless telegraphy ” has the same meaning as in
the Wireless Telegraphy Law.

3. A person shall not work or use any apparatus for
wireless telegraphy installed on a foreign warship whilst that
ship is within the territorial waters of the Colony, otherwise
than in accordance with rules made by the Admiralty from
time to time, and notified in the Gazette, and with Regula-
tions made in that behalf by the Governor in Council.
