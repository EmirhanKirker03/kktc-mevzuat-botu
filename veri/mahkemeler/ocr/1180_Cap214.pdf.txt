A LAW TO PROVIDE FOR THE DISCIPLINE OF THE BRITISH
MILITARY ADMINISTRATION POLICE FORCE AND TO
REGULATE CERTAIN MATTERS “IN CONNECTION THERE-
WITH.

(1944 October, 1944.]

Whereas a force known as the British Military Administra-
tion AT (B) 1 Police and Prisons Service has been formed in
Cyprus under the authority of the Commander-in-Chief,
Middle East Forces, for the purpose of being trained in
Cyprus and thereafter maintaining public order and safety
in territory occupied or to be occupied by the Armed Forces
of Her Majesty (hereinafter referred to as “the British
Military Administration Police Force ”’):

And whereas it is necessary to provide for the discipline of
the British Military Administration Police Force while in
Cyprus and to regulate certain other matters in connection
therewith:

Be it therefore enacted by His Excellency the Officer
Administering the Government and Commander-in-Chief of
the Colony of Cyprus as follows :—

1. This Law may be cited as the British Military
Administration Police Force Discipline Law.

2. In this Law—

“ Army Act ” means the Army Act, 1881 (44 and

45 Vict., c. 58), of the United Kingdom, and includes all

. Acts:amending or substituted for the same and all
articles of war thereunder in force.

3. (1) Notwithstanding anything in the Army Act con-
tained all persons who before the coming into operation of
this Law have agreed or shall hereafter agree to serve in the
British Military Administration Police Force shall, during
their service in Cyprus and while in Cyprus, be subject in
matters concerning discipline and administration to the code
of discipline contained in the Schedule to this Law (herein-
after in this Law referred to as “ the code of discipline.’’)

(2) The officers of the British Military Administration
Police Force may exercise in Cyprus in relation to the
members of the same in matters concerning discipline and
administration all such powers as are conferred on them by
the code of discipline.

(3) Any sentence of imprisonment imposed under the
code of discipline may be served in any prison or detention
barracks in Cyprus.

4. (1) No action, prosecution or other legal proceeding
whatsoever shall be brought, instituted or maintained against
any person holding office or employed in the service of the
British Military Administration Police Force or against any
other person acting under the authority of a person so hold-
ing office or so employed, for the purpose of maintaining
discipline over members of the British Military Administra-
tion Police Force for, or on account of, or in respect of, any
act, matter or thing advised, commanded, ordered, directed
or done in good faith before the coming into operation of this
Law for the maintenance of discipline and good order by
members of the said Force.

(2) Every such person as aforesaid by whom any such act,
matter or thing shall have been advised, commanded,
ordered, directed or done for the purposes aforesaid, shall be
freed, acquitted, discharged, released and indemnified against
all and every person whomsoever in respect thereof.

(3) Every such act, matter or thing referred to in the
preceding subsection shall be presumed to have been
advised, commanded, ordered, directed or done, as the case
may be, in good faith until the contrary shall have been
proved by the party complaining.

CoDE OF DISCIPLINE.

1. In this Schedule—

“Commissioner of the Force” includes its duly appointed deputy;

“Force” means the British Military Administration Police Force;

“member of the Force” means any person who has agreed, whether
before or after the coming into operation of this Law, to serve in the
Force.

2. Any member of the Force who during his service in Cyprus and while
in Cyprus—

(a) fails in any of the duties imposed upon him as a member of such
Force;

(6) wrongfully exceeds or abuses any of the powers conferred upon
him as a member of such Force;

(c) begins, raises, abets, countenances, incites or encourages any
mutiny in such Force;

(d) causes or joins in any sedition or disturbance whatsoever in such
Force;

(e) strikes or offers violence to anyone who is his superior in such
Force;

(f) deserts or assists any other member of such Force to desert;

(g) absents himself from duty without leave;

(h) displays cowardice in the execution of his duty;

(i) fails to deliver up in good order any arms, clothing or other
necessaries, the property of such Force in his charge, when
called upon to do so by any officer under whose command he
is at the time, or wilfully or by culpable negligence destroys,
loses, damages or otherwise disposes of any such articles;

(j) disobeys any of the Standing Orders of the Commissioner of such
Force;

(k) disobeys any lawful order of his superior in such Force;

(0) does any act prejudicial to the good order and discipline of such
Force,

shall be guilty of an offence against police discipline.

3. If reasonable suspicion exists that any police inspector, non-
commissioned officer or constable of the Force has committed an offence
against police discipline, he may be arrested by any officer, or non-
commissioned officer of the Force of higher rank than himself and detained
in any suitable place of detention for a period not exceeding twenty-four
hours pending investigation of the charge by the Commissioner of the
Force, and failing such investigation shall be released.

4. (1) The Commissioner of the Force may summarily inquire into any
offence against police discipline and may—
(a) if the offender is an inspector of the Force, impose on him any one
or more of the following punishments :—

(i) stoppage of pay up to fourteen days’ pay;
