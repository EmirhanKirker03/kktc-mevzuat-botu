To REGULATE THE BURIAL AND EXHUMATION OF CORPSES.
[14th August, 1928.]

1. This Law may be cited as the Public Health (Burial
and Exhumation) Law.

2. A person shall not in any burial ground bury or cause
or suffer to be buried any corpse in such a manner as to
require or allow any part of the coffin or other receptacle
containing the corpse to be placed at a less depth than four
feet below the level of the surface of the ground adjoining
the grave.

3. No person shall exhume any corpse without an Order
of the Governor and except under such terms and conditions
with regard to exhumation and re-burial as may be pre-
scribed in such order.

4, (1) Any person acting in contravention of the
provisions of section 2 of this Law shall be liable on convic-
tion to a fine not exceeding ten pounds.

(2) Any person acting in contravention of the provisions
of section 3 of this Law or of any of the terms and conditions
of an order made thereunder shall be liable on conviction to
imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or to a
fine not exceeding ten pounds or to both.

5. Nothing in this Law contained shall in any way
restrict or be construed to restrict the powers conferred
upon Coroners by the Coroners Law.
