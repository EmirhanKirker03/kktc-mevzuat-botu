A LAW TO PROVIDE FOR A SUMMER AFTERNOON RECESS
IN PLACES OF BUSINESS.

[17th June, 1938.)

1. This Law may be cited as the Summer Afternoon
Kecess Law.

2. In this Law—

“ Commissioner '’ means the Commissioner of Labour
and includes any officer appointed by him for the
purposes of the Law ;

“ place of business " includes any place or premises
in or upon which any trade, industry or business is
carried on.

3. (1) The Governor may by Order in Council to be
published in the Gazette (hereinafter called “a closing
order ”) direct that places of business shall be closed and
kept closed—

(a) during such period betwen the fifteenth day of May
and the thirtieth day of September in any
year ; and

(6) on such days of the week ; and

(c) for such interval between the hours of twelve

(2) Subject to the provisions of section 5 no trade,
industry or business whatsoever shall be carried on in any
place of business affected by a closing order during the
afternoon recess specified in such Order.

4. A closing Order may be made to apply either in
respect of places of business throughout the Colony or to
places of business in any town, village or area specified
therein,

5. Notwithstanding anything contained in any closing
Order the Commissioner may by permit in writing under his
hand for such purpose or in such circumstances as may be
specified therein authorize any place of business to be
opened or kept open during the afternoon recess specified
in any such Order, subject to such terms and conditions as
he may see fit to impose.

6. Where more than one kind of trade, industry or
business is carried on at the same place of business, and any
one kind is such as would, if carried on alone at such place
of business, exempt such place of business from the operation
of a closing Order, such place of business shall not, if other-
wise coming within the operation of any such Order, be
opened or kept open during the afternoon recess except
under a permit in writing obtained from the Commissioner
and subject to such terms and conditions as he may see fit
to impose.

7. Any person who contravenes any provision of this Law
or of any closing Order, or any term or condition contained
in a permit granted by the Commissioner pursuant to
section 5 or 6 shall be guilty of an offence and shall be
liable to a fine of ten pounds.

any place, not being a place of business, retail trade or
