A LAW TO PROVIDE FOR THE TRANSFER OF THE PROPERTY
AND ASSETS OF THE TRUSTEES OF THE St. BARNABAS
SCHOOL FOR THE BLIND TO THE GOVERNOR.

(Ist November, 1957.]

Whereas Dame Louisa Storrs, by deed, made the 6th
day of June, 1932, conveyed to trustees the property in
St. Barnabas School for the Blind (hereinafter called “ the
Blind School ”) together with certain funds to be utilized
for the furtherance of the objects ot the Blind School;

And whereas the Director of Education by the same deed
conveyed to the said trustees certain lands pertaining to the
Blind School;

And whereas the buildings of the said Blind School and
the Lands pertaining thereto were sold by the trustees by
virtue of an Order made by the Supreme Court on the
5th day of July, 1934, and whereas new buildings were
constructed elsewhere on Government Lands;

And whereas during the year 1955, the Loan Commis-
sioners acting under the Public Loans Law, loaned to the
trustees the sum of £7,000 of public money to be used in the
construction of new buildings for the Blind School;

And whereas the trust fund is now insufficient for the
proper operation of the Blind School;

And whereas the Trustees desire to place the Blind School
on a more permanent footing for the benefit of the Blind
of the Colony;

And whereas it is the intention of the Governor to
provide for blind children the facilities which the trust has

hitherto provided and, to this end, to establish and continue
a place for the residence, education and use of blind children:

And whereas the Governor and the Trustees have agreed
that the Blind School, all property pertaining thereto, and
the management and control thereof shall vest in the
Governor;

And whereas doubts have arisen as to the proper mode of
vesting the said property and the management and control
thereof in the Governor,

1. This Law may be cited as the St. Barnabas School for
the Blind (Transfer) Law and shall be deemed to have
come into force on the first day of July, 1957.

2. The ownership of all movable and immovable property
now standing in the name of the Trustees of the St. Barnabas
School for the Blind is hereby vested in the Governor for
the purposes of maintaining a school for the blind in the
name of St. Barnabas and the Governor shall take over
and, subject to the provisions of section 4, carry on the
Blind School.

3. The trust established under a deed made on the 6th
day of June, 1932, between Dame Louisa Storrs, wife of
Sir Ronald Storrs, K.C.M.G., C.B.E, of the first part and
Robert Noel Henry, Acting Director of Education, of the
second part and the Director of Education, the Social
Worker and the Treasurer (therein collectively referred to
as “the Trustees,” which term included the Trustees for
the time being), of the third part, is hereby terminated
and the trustees thereunder are hereby released from their
obligations and duties under the aforesaid trust.

4. (1) The Blind School shall be managed and controlled
by a Board of Management which shall consist of not less
than three fit and proper persons appointed by the
Governor,

(2) The Governor may at any time terminate any
appointment made by him under the provisions of this
section.
