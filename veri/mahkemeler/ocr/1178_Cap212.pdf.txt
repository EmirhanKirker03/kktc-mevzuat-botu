TO EXTEND THE PROVISIONS OF THE ARMY ACTS TO
Cyprus,
[6th June, 1882.)

1. This Law may be cited as the Army Acts Application
Law.

2. The Army Discipline and Regulation Act, 1879, the
Discipline and Regulation (Commencement) Act, 1879, the
Army Discipline and Regulation (Annual) Acts, 1880 and
1881, and the Regulation of the Forces Act, 1881, so far as
the same are not repealed by the Army Act, 1881, the Army
Act, 1881, and every other Act which shall hereafter be from
time to time enacted by the Parliament of the United King-
dom in continuance of or in substitution for the said Acts, or
any of them, shall be of full force and effect within the
Colony.
