4. The Governor in Council may from time to time and at

fit, any district or area, or place within any area, as being no

by such declaration provided, any general or special order
that may have been made under the provisions of this Law
shall, so far as it affects any district, area or place within
any area, cease to have effect.

5. Any person authorized by the Governor may, with or
without any animals or beasts of burthen, carts, tools,
implements or other things, enter upon any land and there
examine any vines, plants or other products of a vegetable
nature, and dig up the ground and do all such other acts and
things as may be expedient in order the more effectually to
search for and destroy phylloxera.

7. When any vines or other vegetable products shall have
been destroyed under the authority of this Law, the
Commissioner of the district shall thereupon pay out of the
Department of the Accountant-General to the owner of the
vines or products compensation for such destruction, such
compensation to be assessed by a valuer or valuers to be
appointed by the Governor for that purpose :

Provided always that if it shall be proved to the satisfac-
tion of the Governor that the destruction of any vines or
other vegetable products was rendered advisable or
necessary by the wilful contravention by the owner of such
vines or products of the provisions of this Law, the
Governor may forbid the payment of such compensation.

8. In order to provide the money that may be required
for carrying into effect the provisions of this Law the
Governor in Council, as soon as any place shall have been
declared to be infected with phylloxera under the provisions
of this Law, may order that there be charged and collected
yearly an addition to the export duty on raisins of not more
than eight per centum thereof, as the Governor in Council
shall from time to time direct.

9. For the purpose of preventing the introduction into
the Colony or the spreading therein of phylloxera, and for
the purpose of making better provision for preventing the
importation of goods deemed likely to convey phylloxera
germs and coming directly or originally from countries or
places believed to be infected with phylloxera, the Governor
may from time to time order that the importation of any
goods or articles be prohibited, or be permitted only on such
conditions as shall be determined by any such order ; and
the Governor may from time to time vary or rescind any
such order.

10. Any person who shall import or bring into the Colony
any goods or articles, or shall do any act or thing, in
contravention of any order made under the last preceding
section shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to a
fine not exceeding fifty pounds or to imprisonment for any
term not exceeding twelve months.; and all such goods or
articles shall be forfeited, together with the packages
containing them and everything packed therewith.
