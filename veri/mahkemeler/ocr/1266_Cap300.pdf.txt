4. (1) The Governor in Council may make Regulations
for the management, control and supervision of aerodromes
to which this Law applies.

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of the powers
conferred by subsection (1), Regulations under this section
may provide for all or any of the following matters:—

(4) the regulation and restriction of the admission of
persons to aerodromes to which this Law applies
or any part thereof;

(4) the regulation and restriction of the use of vehicles,
ships or other objects of any class or description
on any part of an aerodrome to which this Law
apples;

(c) the destruction of livestock trespassing-on any
aerodrome to which this Law applies;

(4) the imposition of penalties not exceeding six months
imprisonment or one hundred pounds fine or
both such imprisonment and fine for any contra-
vention of any of the Regulations.

(3) Regulations made under this section may be general
or may be restricted to any particular aerodrome or aero-
dromes.

5. (1) Where the Governor in Council is satisfied that
any buikling, structure, erection, tree or other thing whatso-
ever on any land within one mile of an aerodrome to which
this Law applies constitutes a danger to aircraft flying in the
vicinity of such aerodrome he may by Order published in
the Gazette authorise, where the aerodrome is a Government
aerodrome, the Director and any person acting under the
orders of the Director, or where the aerodrome is a licensed
aerodrome, the licensee and any person acting under the
order of the licensee —

(a) to remove or to alter to such extent as may be
specified in the Order such building, structure,
erection, tree or thing; and

(0) so far as may be necessary for exercising any of the
powers conferred by the Order to enter upon and
pass over (with or without vehicles) any such
land as may be specified in the Order.

(2) Any person suffering loss or damage in consequence
of an Order made under this section shall be paid compensa-
tion therefor and, in default of agreement, the amount of
such compensation shall be fixed by a single arbitrator
appointed by the Chief Justice.

(3) Where the Order is issued in relation to a Government
aerodrome the compensation shall be paid by the Govern-
ment.

(4) Where the Order is issued in relation to a licensed
aerodrome the compensation shall be payable by the licensee.

(5) Notwithstanding the other provisions of this section
no compensation shall be payable for any loss or damage
suffered in consequence of an Order made under this section
where the building, structure, erection, thing or tree has
been erected or planted in contravention of section 6.

6. (1) Where the Governor in Council is satisfied that,
for the purpose of ensuring the safety of aircraft flying in the
vicinity.ol an aerodrome to which this Law applies, it is
expedient so to do, he may make an Order (hereinafter
referred to as a “ restricting Order ”) under this section.

(2) A restricting Order shall be published in the Gazette
and shall relate to any area specilied in the Order being
within one mile of the boundaries of an aerodrome to which
this Law applies.

(3) A restricting Order may provide
(a) either for the prohibition, or the restriction, or both
such prohibition and restriction, of the erection
of buildings, structures or other things or any
class of buildings, structures or other things on
the area specified in the Order or any part of

such area;

(b) either for the prohibition, or the restriction, or both
such prohibition and restriction, of the planting
of any trees or class of trees in the area specified
in the Order or any part thereof.

(4) Any person who contravenes an Order made under
this section shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine
not exceeding one hundred pounds.

(5) No compensation shall be payable for any loss or
damage suffered in consequence of an Order made under this
section whereby a restriction only is imposed.

(6) Any person suffering loss or damage in consequence
of an Order made under this section, whereby a prohibition
or a prohibition and restriction are imposed, shall be paid
compensation therefor and, in default of agreement, the
amount of such compensation shall be fixed by single
arbitrator appointed by the Chief Justice.
