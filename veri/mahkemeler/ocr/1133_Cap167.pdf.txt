A LAW TO PROVIDE FOR THE MANAGEMENT AND CONTROL
OF THE ENGLISH SCHOOL,

[6th December, 1935. |

Whereas the Reverend Frank Darvall Newham, Officer
of the Most Excellent Order of the British Empire, Master
of Arts, Honorary Canon of St. George’s Cathedral,
Jerusalem, (in this Law called “the Grantor" which
expression, Where the context so admits, shall include his
heirs, executors, administrators and assigns) founded in
the year 1900 at Nicosia an educational establishment
known as the English School (in this Law called “ the
English School "’):

And whereas the Grantor has at his own cost and
expense maintained and managed the English School since
the date of its foundation and has endowed it with property
of considerable value:

And whereas by an Indenture dated the 12th day of
May, 1930, (in this Law called “ the Indenture "’), it was
agreed to the effect following, that is to say—

(a) that in consideration of the sum of six thousand
pounds to be paid to the Grantor by a Board
of Trustees therein named (in this Law called
“the Board”) the Grantor would grant and
convey to the Board all the immovable property
described in the Indenture;

(b) that the Grantor would, upon payment of the said
sum of six thousand pounds, deliver to the

Board all the movable property forming the
furniture and equipment of the English School;

(c) that, upon payment of the said sum of six thousand
pounds, the Board should take over the manage-
ment and control of the English School and
should manage and control the same in manner
prescribed by the Indenture:

And whereas the immovable property described in the
Indenture has been conveyed to and is now vested in the
Board:

And whereas out of the said sum of six thousand pounds
a sum of four thousand and five hundred pounds has been
paid to the Grantor by the Board:

And whereas the Grantor and the Board desire to place
the English School on a more permanent foundation for the
benefit of the youth of the Colony:

And whereas the Governor has paid to the Grantor the
sum of one thousand and five hundred pounds, being the
balance of the said sum of six thousand pounds due to the
Grantor:

And whereas the Grantor, the Board and the Governor
have agreed that the said movable and immovable property
and the management and control of the English School
should vest in the Governor:

And whereas doubts have arisen as to the proper mode of
vesting the said movable and immovable property and the
management and control of the English School in the
Governor:

Be it therefore enacted :—

1, This Law may be cited as the English School (Manage-
ment and Control) Law.

2. (1) The ownership of the immovable property now
standing registered in the name of the Board under two
Certificates of Registration bearing numbers 510 and 606,
and dated the 15th day of November, 1930, is hereby vested
in the Governor, and the Director of Lands and Surv evs
is hereby empowered to register the said immovable
property in the name of the Governor.

(2) Subject to the provisions of section 5, the Governor
may at any time after registration thereof in his name sell,
exchange or otherwise dispose of the said immovable
property or any part thereof.

3. (1) Within one month from the date of a notification
to the Grantor by or on behalf of the Governor, not being
later than the Ist day of August, 1936, the Grantor shall
assign and deliver to the Governor all the movable property
forming the furniture and equipment of the English School
at the date of the notification, whereupon the Governor—

(a) shall take over, and shall continue to carry on, the
English School as a school in its present form
or in such other form as the Governor may from
time to time determine:

Provided that—

(i) the chief medium of instruction in the
school shall be English;

(ii) the character of the school shall be
Christian but undenominational and all pupils
(including Moslems) shall have facilities for
practising their own form of religion;

(b) shall manage and control the English School as in
section 4 prescribed.

(2) Pending the assignment and delivery of the movable
property to the Governor as in subsection (1) provided the
Grantor shall continue to carry on the English School as
heretofore and shall be responsible for the management and
maintenance thereof,

(3) Subject to the provisions of section 5, the Governor
may at any time after the assignment and delivery thereof
sell, exchange or otherwise dispose of the said movable
property or any part thereof.

4. (1) The English School, either in its present form or
in such modified form as may be determined by the
Governor under the provisions of section 3 (1) (a), shall be
managed and controlled by a Board of Management
consisting of a Chairman and eight other members, who
shall be fit and proper persons appointed by the Governor:

Provided that no less than four of the number of such
nine persons shall be former pupils of the English School,

(2) The Governor may at any time terminate the appoint-
ment of any member of the Board of Management.

(3) The Governor may upon the death, resignation,
retirement or termination of appointment of any member
of the Board of Management appoint another fit and
proper person to fill the vacancy.

(4) The Governor may in the case of illness or absence
from the Colony of any member of the Board of Management
appoint a fit and proper person in the place of such member
until such member shall resume his duties.

(5) The Board of Management shall conduct its meetings
and proceedings and shall manage and control the English
School in accordance with regulations made by it and
approved by the Governor.

5. (1) If at any time after the Board of Management has

assumed the management and control of the English E
School the Governor, on the advice of the Board of Manage- *

ment, considers it no longer necessary for the English
School to continue to function as a school, the Governor
may, by writing under his hand, direct that the English
School be closed, and thereupon it shall be closed,

(2) Upon the closing of the English School as in sub-
section (1) provided the Governor may, by writing under
his hand, direct—

(a) that such part of the immovable and movable
property mentioned in sections 2 and 3 as may
remain undisposed of at the date of the closing
be utilized for such purpose being for the
benefit of the youth of the Colony as the
Governor may determine, or be sold and the
proceeds thereof be applied to such purpose
aforesaid and in such manner as the Governor
may determine;

(6) that the proceeds of such part of the said im-
movable and movable property as may have
been disposed of before the date of the closing
be utilized in the like manner.
