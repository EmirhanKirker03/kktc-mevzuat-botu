A LAW TO PROVIDE FOR THE INVESTIGATION OF THE AFFAIRS
AND FOR THE AUDITING OF THE ACCOUNTS OF THE
CHURCHES AND MONASTERIES OF THE AUTOCEPHALOUS
GREEK-ORTHODOX CHURCH OF CYPRUS IN CERTAIN
_ CASES.
1949 Cap. 60. [1st October, 1937.]

Short title. 1. This Law may be cited as the Churches and Monasteries
of the Autocephalous Greek-Orthodox Church of Cyprus
(Investigation and Audit) Law.

(a) the production before him of all books and
documents in the custody or power of the com-
mittee or council concerned;

(b) any person holding or accountable for any such
books or documents to appear before him at any
such investigation or audit, or at any such

(2) Any person who is required to answer questions put
