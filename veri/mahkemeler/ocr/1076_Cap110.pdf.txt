10. Any school or other institution maintained by any
recognized society or any person under a permit under this
Law may be inspected by the Governor or such person as
he may appoint in order to ensure that the spirit of such
school or other institution is satisfactory.

11. Notwithstanding anything in the Medical Registra-
tion Law to the contrary contained, no alien medical
practitioner, not being a missionary or employee or member
of a recognized society, shall be entitled in any Court to
recover any charge for any medical or surgical* advice or
attendance or for the performance of any operation or for
any medicine which he has prescribed and supplied unless
he was at the relevant date in possession of a permit under
this Law.

12. (1) Any person who engages in philanthropic, educa-
tional or medical work in contravention of this Law shall
be guilty of an offence and shall be liable to imprisonment
for a term not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding
one hundred pounds or to both.

(2) In any case in which a person is convicted of having
engaged in medical work without being in possession of a
permit under this Law the Registrar of the Court before
which he has been convicted shall forward a certified copy
of the conviction to the Registrar appointed under
section 5 of the Medical Registration Law, or any amend-
ment thereof, who shall thereupon erase from the Medical
Register the name of such convicted person if his name
appears therein.

SCHEDULE.
(Section 7.)

FORM OF UNDERTAKING.

I, , hereby undertake to pay all due obedience and respect to
the Government of Cyprus, and while carefully abstaining from participa-
tion in political affairs, I desire and purpose ex animo to work in friendly
co-operation with the said Government in all matters in which my influence
may properly be exerted; and in particular, I undertake, if engaged in
educational work, that my influence shall be exerted to promote loyalty
to the Government of Cyprus in the minds of my pupils, and to make them
good citizens of the British Empire.

(Signed)
