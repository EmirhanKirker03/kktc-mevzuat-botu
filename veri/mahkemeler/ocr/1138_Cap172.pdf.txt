A LAW TO MAKE PROVISION FOR THE PAYMENT OF PENSION ,

GRATUITIES AND RELIEF IN RESPECT OF CERTAIN
PERSONAL INJURIES SUSTAINED DURING THE PERIOD
OF THE PRESENT EMERGENCY.

[3rd September, 1939. |

1. This Law may be cited as the Civil Defence Organiza-
tion (Emergency Provisions) Law.

2. In this Law—

“ civil defence organization ’”’ means any organiza-
tion established for civil defence purposes which is
declared by the Governor to be a civil defence organiza-
tion for the purposes of this Law;

“civil defence volunteer,” in relation to an injury,
means a person certified by the Officer to have been a
member of a civil defence organization at the time the
injury was sustained ;

“ Ofücer ” means the person appointed by the
Governor to be in charge of any civil defence organiza-
tion ;

“period of the present emergency ” means the
period commencing on the 3rd September, 1939, and
ending on such date as the Governor in Council declares
to be the date on which the emergency that was the
occasion of the passing of this Law came to an end;

“ war service injury,” in relation to a civil defence
volunteer, means any physical injury which is shown

arisen out of and in the performance by the volunteer
of his duties as a member of the civil defence organiza-

course of his employment in any other capacity.

3. The Governor may during the period of the present
emergency by notice in the Gazette declare any organiza-
tion established in the Colony for civil defence purposes to
be a civil defence organization for the purposes of this Law
and may appoint any person to be Officer in charge of any
such organization.

4. Any civil defence volunteer who sustains any war
service injury shall receive such pension, gratuity or
temporary incapacity allowance as the Governor in Council
may determine, chargeable upon the revenue of the Colony.

5. (1) The widow or family of any civil defence volunteer
who has died as the direct result of a war service injury shall
receive such pension or gratuity as the Governor in Council
may determine, chargeable upon the revenue of the Colony:

Provided that no pension or gratuity shall be paid to an
orphan brother or sister of a deceased civil defence volunteer
if a pension or gratuity has been paid to any of the persons
enumerated in paragraphs (a) and (6) of the definition of
“ family ” contained in subsection (2) of this section:

Provided further that no such pension or gratuity shall be
payable if such war service injury was sustained more than
seven years before the death of such volunteer.
