A LAW TU MAKE PROVISION FOR THE SECURITY OF PUBLIC
BUILDINGS AND FOR SEARCHING OF PERSONS ENTERING
THEREIN AND FOR OTHER MATTERS CONNECTED THERE-
WITH AND SIMILAR THERETO,

{12th October, 1955.1

1. This Law may be cited as the Public Buildings
(Security) Law. .

2. In this Law, unless the context otherwise requires—
“ means of conveyance '' means any animal, carriage,
railway waggon, bicycle, motor vehicle of any descrip-
tion or any other vehicle used for the purpose of
conveyance of persons or goods ;
public building ’’ means any building belonging to,
or for the time being used by, the Government or Her
Majesty's Forces, or any Department or branch thereof,
and includes any building belonging to, or used by, any
statutory body or authority, or any corporation,
company or other person performing a public utility
service.

3. Without prejudice to any other powers conferred by
any other Law and notwithstanding anything in any other
Law contained, no person shall—

la) enter any public building, or

(b) pass any barrier set up at any place by the Police

Force or by Her Majesty's Forces,

unless he first submits, if required so to do by any police
officer or any member of Her Majesty’s Forces, to a search,
by such police officer or such member of Her Majesty’s
Forces, of his person, and of anything carried by or on him,
and of any means of conveyance accompanying him,
