TO DECLARE CERTAIN DAYS TO BE BANK HOLIDAYS.

[12th May, 1928.)

1. This Law may be cited as the Bank Holidays Law.

2. “ Non-büsiness ” day means any day prescribed by
this Law as a bank holiday. Any other day is a business
day.

3. A bank holiday shall be a day kept as a close holiday
in all banks in Cyprus ; and all bills of exchange, promissory
notes and other negotiable instruments which are due
and payable on any such bank holiday shall be payable
and in case of non-payment may be protested, on the
next following business day ; and any such protest shall
be as valid as if made on the day on which the bill or
note was made due and payable ; and for all the purposes
of this Law the day next following a bank holiday shall
mean the next following day upon which a bill of exchange
may be lawfully protested.

4. When the day on which any bill of exchange, or
other negotiable instrument should be presented or any
notice relating thereto should be given is a bank holiday,
such bill shall be presented or such notice given on the
business day next following such bank holiday.

substituted day and shall cease to apply to any day for
which another has been so substituted.
