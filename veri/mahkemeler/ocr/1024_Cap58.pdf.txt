1949 Cap. 87. {27th October, 1909.)
Short title, 1. This Law may be cited as the Fish Preservation Law,
Interpreta- 2. In this Law—

Sone “fish ” means all kinds of fish and includes eels.

Taking fish 3. No person shall by any means whatever kill, capture or

orang pursue or aid in killing, capturing or pursuing any fish in any

unlawful in of the reservoirs, channels, drains and other waters con-

e. nected with the public irrigation works in the District of
Famagusta without the written permission of the Director
of Water Development.

Venalty. 4. Any person acting in contravention of the provisions of

this Law shall be guilty of an offence under this Law and
shall be liable upon summary conviction before a Court of
Magisterial jurisdiction to a fine not exceeding one pound
or in default of payment thereof to a period of imprisonment
not exceeding one week.
