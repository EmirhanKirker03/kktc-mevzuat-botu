A LAW TO REGULATE NIGHT WORK IN BAKERIES.
(16th January, 1950. |
This Law may be cited as the Bakeries (Night Work)

Law.

2. In this Law, unless the context otherwise requires—

“ bakery * means any place in which bread, pastry
or other flour confectionery is made or manufactured
by way of trade or for purposes of gain;

‘Commissioner * means the Commissioner of
Labour;

“inspector * means an inspector of the Labour
Department;

“work incidental ” in relation to the making or
manufacture of bread, pastry or other flour con-
fectionery includes slicing, wrapping, or otherwise
preparing bread or flour confectionery for sale or
distribution, but save as aforesaid does not include
work in or incidental to distribution.

3. Subject to the provisions of this Law, no person shall
engage or shall employ any other person in the making
during cer- or manufacture of bread, pastry or other flour confectionery
or in any other work incidental thereto in any bakery
between the hours of ten o'clock in the evening and five
o'clock in the following morning, except on Mondays
when the hour shall be four o’clock in the morning
instead of five o'clock:

Provided that the Governor may, by notification in the
Gazette, vary the aforesaid hours between the first day of
June and the thirtieth day of September in any year as
may be specified in the notification but so that there shall
always be an interval of seven consecutive hours between
such hours:

Provided further that this section shall not apply where,
owing to a sudden breakdown of machinery or plant,
it is necessary to complete the work during the prohibited
hours.

4. The Governor may by Order provide that the
restrictions imposed by this Law shall not apply, or shall
apply subject to such modifications as may be specified
in the Order, in respect of work in bakeries—

(4) in the preparation of yeast for bread-making;

(6) for the purpose ol providing supplies for public
holidays and feast days;

(6) for the purpose of providing supplies to meet
seasonal demands in holiday resorts;

(4) in connection with employment for the purpose
of meeting a sudden and unexpected demand
for bread or any other unforeseen emergency;
and

{e) in any other circumstances or for any other purpose
in or for which the Governor is satisfied that it is
desirable that special provision for work at night
should be made,

5. The Commissioner and any inspector or police officer
may enter any bakery between the hours during which
work in bakeries is prohibited under the provisions of this
Law for the purposes of inspection and for ascertaining
whether any breach of this Law or of any Order made
thereunder has been or is being committed therein or
thereon and the owner and the person for the time being
in charge of any such bakery shall provide all reasonable
facilities for such entry and inspection.

6. Any person who—

(a) contravenes or fails to comply with the provisions
of section 3 or of any notification or Order made
under this Law;
