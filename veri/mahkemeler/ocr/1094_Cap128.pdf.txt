A LAW TO PROVIDE FOR THE FREE IMPORTATION OF CRUDE
ASBESTOS FOR THE PURPOSE OF RE-EXPORT AFTER
PROCESSING IN THE COLONY.

1. This Law may be cited as the Asbestos (Processing
and Exportation) Law.

2. In this Law, unless the context otherwise requires—

“ crude asbestos’ means asbestos-bearing rock in
its natural state which has not undergone any form of
mechanical treatment ;

“ processing ” means the separation of the asbestos
fibres from the rock and grading such fibres according
to their lengths.

3. Notwithstanding anything in any other Law contained
crude asbestos imported from any place outside the Colony
for the purpose of re-export after processing in the Colony
shall be admitted into the Colony free of import duty.

4. Upon the export from the Colony, after processing,
of any asbestos imported free of duty as in section 3
provided, there shall be collected and paid a duty of three

er centum calculated on the value thereof delivered on
board of the vessel on which such asbestos is being exported.

5. (1) The Governor in Council may make Regulations to
be published in the Gazette generally for carrying out the
purposes of this Law and in particular for—

(a) prescribing the books and accounts to be kept by

importers of crude asbestos for the purpose of
checking the quantity imported and the quantity

of asbestos exported after processing and pro- .

viding for the inspection of such books and
accounts ;
