Short title 1. This Law may be cited as the Unauthorized Uniforms
Law.

“Her Majesty's Naval, Military or Air Forces”
includes reserve and auxiliary forces ;

‘other uniform " means any distinctive dress which
indicates or is intended to indicate or may be taken to
indicate that the person wearing it belongs to, or is a
member of, any political association, institution,
organisation or party.

3. Any person who—

(a) not being a person serving in Her Majesty's Naval,
Military or Air Forces, wears or uses, without
permission in writing from the Governor, any
uniform authorised for use by Her Majesty's
Naval, Military or Air Forces or any uniform so
nearly resembling the same as to be likely to
deceive ; or

(b) not being a person serving in the Cyprus Police
Force, or other Department, wears or uses,
without permission in writing from the Governor,
any uniform authorised for use by the Cyprus
Police Force or other Department or any
uniform so nearly resembling the same as to be
likely to deceive ; or

(¢) Wears or uses, without permission in writing from
the Governor, any other uniform,

shall be guilty of an offence.
Penalty : five pounds fine.

4. Any person who, not being a person serving in Her
Majesty's Naval, Military or Air Forces or in the Cyprus
Police Force, or other Department, wears the uniform of
any of these Forces or Department, or any dress having
the appearance or bearing any of the regimental or other
distinctive marks of any such uniform, or any other
uniform whatsoever, in such manner or in such circumstances
as to be likely to bring contempt on that uniform, or employs
any other person so to wear such uniform or dress, shall
be guilty of an offence.

Penalty : three months imprisonment,

5. Where under this Law any act, if done without
lawful permission is an offence, the burden of proving that
the act was done with lawful permission shall lie upon the
person accused.

6. It shall be lawful for the Governor in granting any
permission under this Law to limit the area within which
the uniform the subject of such permission may be worn,

and generally to qualify such permission in such other
manner as he may think fit,

7. Nothing in this Law contained shall prevent—

(a) any person from wearing or using any uniform in
the course of a stage play performed in any
place in which stage plays may lawfully be
publicly performed or in the course of a music
hall or circus performance or in the course of
any bona fide naval, military or air force
representation ; or

(6) any person from wearing or using any uniform
authorised by any other Law,
