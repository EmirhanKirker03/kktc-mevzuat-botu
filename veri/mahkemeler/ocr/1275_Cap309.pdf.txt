Short title. 1. This Law may be cited as the Nursing Sisters (Pre-

servation of Rights) Law.

Preservation 2, Nothing in the repeal of the Nursing Sisters Pensions
Sizi» Law, 1928, shall affect—

(a) any retiring allowance granted thereunder to any
matron or nursing sister;

(b) the grant of a retiring allowance thereunder to any
matron or nursing sister who ceased to serve
under the Government of the Colony prior to
such repeal and who would, but for such repeal,
be eligible on her ultimate retirement to receive
a retiring allowance under the provisions thereof
in respect of her service under the Government
of the Colony.
