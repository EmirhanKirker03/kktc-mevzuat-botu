Short title. 1. This Law may be cited as the Goats (Free Ranging)
Tax Law.

“ free ranging goat ” means any goat not being a goat

the same in any town or village declared to be a

3. There shall be levied and paid for every free ranging
goat a tax of one hundred mils.

4. The tax shall be annual and shall be payable by the
owner in respect of every free ranging goat belonging to him
and enumerated under the Sheep, Goats and Pigs (Enumera-
tion) Law, or any Law amending or substituted for the
same.

5. The tax shall be payable on such date or dates during
the year of enumeration as the Governor may direct by
order published in the Gazette:

Provided that whenever the enumeration takes place
after the İst day of April in any year, the Governor may
direct that the tax payable in respect of free ranging goats
so enumerated shall be paid in the year following the year
of such enumeration and such tax shall be deemed to be the
tax leviable in respect of such goats for the year in which it
is directed that it should be paid.

6. The Governor may, by order published in the Gazette,
suspend the operation of this Law.
