A LAW TO PROVIDE FOR THE INCORPORATION OF AN
ORPHANAGE AND TRAINING SCHOOL ESTABLISHED BY
DEMETRAKIS G. DIANELLOS OF LARNACA.

1 of 50. [11th January, 1950.J

WHEREAS Demetrakis G. Dianellos of Larnaca (in this
Law referred to as “the settlor,” which expression where
the context so admits includes his heirs, executors, ad-
ministrators and assigns), has, by trust deeds made on
the 15th and 20th days of Decembér, 1949, declared that he
holds as a trustee the immovable properties described in
the Schedule hereto together with a sum of fifty thousand
pounds in cash in trust for poor orphan children of the
Greek-Orthodox community for the establishment of an
orphanage and training school for such children:

AND WHEREAS it is expedient to provide by Law for the
establishment of the orphanage and training school as
aforesaid, and for the vesting of the said immovable property
and money in such orphanage and training school and the
management and control thereof:

Short title. 1. This Law may be cited as the Orphanage and Training
School (Demetrakis G. Dianellos of Larnaca) Law.

7. (1) The person or board managing the Orphanage and
Training School may make regulations for the better
carrying out of the purposes of this Law:

Provided that such regulations shall be subject to the
approval of the Governor and shall not come into operation
until approved by him and published in the Gazette.

(2) In particular and without prejudice to the generality
of the powers contained in subsection (1), such regulations
may—

(a) make provision in respect of all matters stated or
required in this Law to be prescribed;

(b) provide for the mode of convening the meetings of
the board, the number required to form a
quorum and the procedure to be followed
thereat ;

(c) prescribe rules for the internal management of
the Orphanage and Training School;

(d) provide for any matter in respect of which any
person or board managing the Orphanage and
Training School may exercise any power or
perform any duty under this Law.

8. The Supreme Court and the Attorney-General shall
have and exercise, in respect of the Orphanage and Training
School, the same powers vested in and exercised by them
respectively under the Charities Law, as if the Orphanage
and Training School is a charity registered and incorporated
under the provisions of the said Law and any Rules of Court
made under the provisions of the said Laws shall apply to
any proceedings taken in respect of any matter arising under
this Law.
