A Law To REGULATE THE USE OF MARBLE IN THE
MANUFACTURE OF ARTICLES.

(14th June, 1935.)

1. This Law may be cited as the Marble Manufacture
(Regulation) Law.

2. In this Law—

“ancient monument “ means any object, building or
site which is or has been declared to be an ancient
monument under any Law relating to antiquities in
force for the time being ;

‘ Director © means the Director of Antiquities.

3. (1) No person shall manufacture any article in the
composition of which marble is used unless he first obtains
a licence from the Director.

(2) Any person to whom a licence has been refused by
the Director may, within ten days from the date of such
refusal, appeal to the Governor in Council whose decision
thereon shall be final and conclusive.

(3) All licences granted under this section shall be issued
free of any charge and shall expire on the 31st day of
December next following the date of issue.

4. Every person to whom a licence has been granted under
section 3 of this Law shall keep a book in such form as
the Director may prescribe wherein such person shall enter

5. Any person found in possession of marble which is
reasonably suspected of being marble taken from an
ancient monument shall, unless he establishes to the satis-
faction of the Court that such marble has not been taken
from an ancient monument, be guilty of an offence and
shall be liable to imprisonment not exceeding six months
or to a fine not exceeding fifty pounds or to both and
any marble in respect of which the offence has been
mitted shall be forfeited.

6. Any person who—

(a) acts in contravention of subsection (1) of section 3
of this Law ;

(c) knowingly makes a false entry in a book kept in
accordance with the provisions of section 4 of
this Law; or

(d) is found in possession of marble which it is reason-
