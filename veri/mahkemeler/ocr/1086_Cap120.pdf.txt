TO AMEND AND CONSOLIDATE THE LAW MAKING PROVISION

FOR CERTAIN GUARANTEES RELATING TO THE AGRI-

CULTURAL BANK OF CYPRUS LIMITED.
[18th January, 1929.]

1. This Law may be cited as the Agricultural Bank of
Cyprus Limited Guarantee Law.

2. (1) It shall be lawful for the Governor to enter into
an agreement guaranteeing that the Agricultural Bank of
Cyprus Limited shall receive a profit not exceeding four
per centum per annum after deducting all expenses, in
respect of all loans made by the said Bank in relief of
agriculture :

Provided always that in the event of the total amount of
such loans at any one time exceeding the sum of fifty
thousand pounds the said guarantee shall only cover the
said sum of fifty thousand pounds and not the excess.

(2) It shall be lawful for the Governor to enter into a
supplemental agreement, on such terms and conditions as
- the Governor with the sanction of the Secretary of State
may determine, guaranteeing payment of interest on behalf
of the Agricultural Bank of Cyprus Limited at a rate not
exceeding five per centum per annum on bonds of an
aggregate nominal value of two hundred thousand pounds
issued by the said Bank for the purpose of making loans in
Telief of agriculture :

Provided always that the total amount of the said
guarantee shall not in any one year exceed the sum of ten
thousand pounds.

3. The Governor may, from time to time, and as occasion
may require, order that a sum of money be appropriated
out of the general revenues of the Colony to meet any
liability due under any guarantee given under section 2 of
this Law.
