MANAGEMENT OF THE CYPRUS PUBLIC LIBRARY.

8. The Committee shall have the absolute management
and control of the Library and of its funds, and may apply
any part of such funds to the purchase of such books,
magazines, periodicals, newspapers, prints, pamphlets,
manuscripts, reports, pictures, maps, engravings, works of
art and science, scientific, philosophical, and other instru-
ments, specimens of natural history, science, art, agriculture,
mining and industry, and all other articles, furniture, and
effects, as they may deem requisite for the purposes of the
Library, and may also sell or otherwise dispose of any
article purchased or acquired by the Library; and all
moneys arising from any such sale or other disposal shall
be deemed to be part of the funds of the Library and shall
be applied accordingly.

9. The Committee shall out of the funds of the Library,
pay all rents, rates, taxes, insurance premiums on buildings
and contents, salaries and wages, costs of additions, repairs,
and other outgoings and expenses payable in respect of the
management of the Library, and the insurance premiums
upon all books and other articles contained in the Library
against loss or damage by fire or otherwise, and the expenses
incidental to all sales under the last preceding section.

10. Any person subscribing to the Library such annual or
other sum as may from time to time be prescribed shall be
deemed to be a subscriber and shall have his name entered
as such in the Register of Subscribers to the Library.

11. Any person making a donation to the Library either
in money, books, or other publications, works of art or
science, or any other articles, of such value as may be pre-
scribed, may, by resolution of the Committee, be elected as

a life subscriber and shall thenceforth be entitled to all the
privileges of an annual subscriber without any further

payment.

12. A subscriber shall be entitled to borrow from the
Library such a number of books, periodicals, newspapers
and magazines and to keep the same for such period as may
from time to time be prescribed, and he shall be responsible
for the proper care of such books, periodicals, newspapers or
magazines and for their due return to the Library.

13. The Committee shall have the power to refuse to
accept, and after acceptance shall have the power to return,
the whole or part of the subscription of any person whom
they may have reason to suspect to be suffering from any
communicable disease, or who may be residing in the same
house with any person suffering or suspected to be suffering
from any such disease; and, upon the return of such sub-
scription, or proportionate part thereof computed for the
unexpired portion of the term for which the subscription
was paid, such person shall immediately cease to be a
subscriber and to enjoy the rights and privileges of a sub-
scriber, and shall be required forthwith to return all books,
periodicals and magazines which may be in his possession.

14. The Committee shall have power to appoint one or
more sub-committees, consisting of such one or more
members as they may think fit, for the purpose of consider-
ing and reporting upon such questions and matters as may
be referred to them, and the Committee may grant to any
such sub-committee the power to do any of the acts relating
to the management of the Library which the Committee
themselves might lawfully do.

15. The Committee may appoint, upon such terms as they
may think fit, a Librarian and such other officers and
servants as they may deem requisite for the purpose of
managing and carrying on the Library; and may fix the

salary and wages to be paid to them, and may at any time
remove any such Librarian or officers or servants.

16. The Committee shall, subject to such regulations as
they may deem fit to make, provide and set apart some
portion of the Library for use as a free reading room, to
which the general public shall have free access to use the

19. If any subscriber shall fail, neglect, or refuse to return
any book, periodical, newspaper or magazine belonging to
the Library within such period as may have been prescribed,
it shall be lawful for the Librarian to deliver to such sub-
scriber a written notice requiring him to return such book,
periodical, newspaper or magazine within seven days from
the date of such notice and to pay any fine which he may
have incurred in respect of such failure, neglect or refusal.
If any subscriber shall fail to comply with such notice
within the time therein specified, he shall be liable to a
penalty not exceeding two pounds in addition to the value
of the book, periodical, newspaper or magazine and to the
amount of the fine incurred.

20. Any person who, in any part of the Library—

(a) behaves in a disorderly manner; or

(0) uses violent, abusive or obscene language; or

(c) bets or gambles; or

(d) after proper warning, persists in remaining in the

Library or reading room beyond the hour fixed
for the closing thereof,

shall be liable to a penalty of two pounds.

towards the maintenance of the Library such moneys as

may from time to time be appropriated to the Library by
the Governor.

22. There shall be paid out of the revenue of the Municipal
Corporation of Nicosia towards the maintenance of the
Library such moneys as may from time to time be appro-

24. The accounts of the Library shall be audited by the
Director of Audit and a yearly statement of account, in such
form as the Director of Audit may direct, shall be forwarded
to the Administrative Secretary. .

25. All penalties imposed by this Law and all fines
prescribed by regulations made under the provisions of
this Law shall be recoverable on summary conviction,
and proceedings may be instituted and conducted by the
Librarian or by any person authorized by the Committee,
either generally or for any particular proceedings.

26. All penalties and fines recovered under this Law or
any regulations made thereunder shall be paid to the
Committee.
