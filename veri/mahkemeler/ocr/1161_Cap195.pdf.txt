20. All actions where any estate is claimed on the ground
of the incapacity of a person to succeed thereto shall be
commenced before the lapse of three years from the date of
the death of the person to whose estate he would otherwise
have succeeded.

48. (1) The degree of kindred between any two persons
shall be ascertained as follows, that is to say, when the two
persons are in the direct line of descent the one from the
other. by reckoning the number of generations from either
of them to the other, each generation constituting a degree ;
and where they are not in the direct line of descent the one
from the other, by reckoning the number of generations
from either of them up to their common ancestor and from
the common ancestor downwards to the other of them, each
generation constituting a degree.
