NHN

For ESTABLISHING A RULE OF THE ROAD FOR PERSONS IN
CHARGE OF VEHICLES AND ANIMALS.

1949

Cap. 149. [12th April, 1899.)
Short title. 1. This Law may be cited as the Rule of the Road Law.
Rules of 2. Every person driving any vehicle, which term in this

Law includes a bicycle or tricycle, or driving or riding or
leading any animal—

(a) when he meets any other vehicle or any animal,
shall keep his own vehicle or animal to the left
side; and ;

(6) when he overtakes and passes any other vehicle or
animal, shall keep his own vehicle or animal to
the right side; and

(c) when he is being overtaken and passed or about to
be overtaken and passed by any vehicle or by
any animal, shall keep his own vehicle or animal
to the left side.

Penalty for 3, Any person who contravenes the provisions of the
foregoing section, except in case of actual necessity, or for
other sufficient reason, shall be liable to a fine not exceeding
five hundred mils.
