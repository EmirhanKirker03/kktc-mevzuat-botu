A Law To REGULATE THE ACTIVITIES OF Boy Scouts
IN CYPRUS:

1947 Cap. 50, [10th March, 1933. |

Whereas by the Royal Charter of its incorporation
granted on the fourth day of January, 1912, the Boy Scouts
Association is inler alta empowered to develop its activities
in the British Overseas Dominions |

And whereas it is expedient to make statutory provision
for the more assured exercise of such power and for the
furtherance and protection of the purposes of the said
Association in Cyprus and for the prevention of the establish-
ment of unauthorized Boy Scouts Associations :

Be it therefore enacted :—

Law.

CAP. 34.) BOY SCOUTS ASSOCIATION.

7. (1) No group, troop or body of foreign boy scouts shall
be formed or organized or shall function in Cyprus under the
constitution, bye-laws or rules of a foreign Association
without a permit from the Governor previously obtained.

(2) The Governor may grant or refuse to grant such
permit or may grant it subject to such terms and conditions
as he thinks fit.

8. No person shall form, organize or work in connection
with, or be concerned in forming, organizing or working in
connection with any group, troop or body of foreign boy
scouts or any group, troop or body whether known as boy
scouts or by any other name, which in its organization and
aims is similar or analogous thereto unless a permit from the
Governor for the formation of such group, troop or body has
been previously obtained,

9, No British subject shall be a member of any group,
troop or body of foreign boy scouts and any person who
induces a British subject to become a member of any such
group, troop or body or who enrolls him as such, shall be
guilty of an offence.

10. In any proceedings against any person for an offence
under this Law—

(a) any document tendered by the prosecution and
purporting to contain the whole or any part of
the constitution, bye-laws or rules of the
Association or of any foreign Association and
purporting to be issued by the Association, or
foreign Association, as the case may, or by the
Boy Scouts’ International Bureau, shall be
prima facie evidence of the contents thereof;

(b) the burden of proving that a permit has been
granted shall lie wpon such person.

11. No prosecution under this Law shall instituted except
by or with the consent of the Attorney-General.

12. Any person contravening any of the provisions of this
Law or failing to observe any of the terms or conditions of a
permit granted under section 7 (2) shall be guilty of an
offence and shall be liable upon conviction to a fine of ten
pounds or to imprisonment for three months or to both,
