shall be entitled to practise medicine, surgery and midwifery
and to demand, suc for, and recover reasonable charges for

medicine or any medical or surgical appliance supplied by

23. No person shall practise medicine, surgery or mid-
wifery or shall be entitled to recover any fee or charge for

being registered under this Law.
