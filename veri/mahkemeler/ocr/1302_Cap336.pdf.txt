To PROVIDE FOR THE CARRYING OF LIGHTS UPON VEHICLES
AFTER SUNSET.
Cop. 181. [31st August, 1909.]
Short title. 1. This Law may be cited as the Vehicles Lights Law.
Interpre- 2. In this Law—
“road ” means any road opened for wheeled traffic;
“ vehicle’? means any -carriage, cart, motor car,
cycle or other conveyance constructed for conveying
persons or goods. .
Carrying 3. Every person in charge of any vehicle on any road shall
night, oo Carryor exhibit a sufficient light between half an hour after
2 of 19/41. sunset and half an hour before sunrise:
Provided that this section shall not apply to a person
leading a bicycle.
Penalty. 4. Any person acting in contravention of this Law shall be

liable to a penalty not exceeding five hundred mils.
