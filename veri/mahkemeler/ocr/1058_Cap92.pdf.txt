A LAW TO CONSOLIDATE AND AMEND THE LAWS AND
REGULATIONS RELATING TO THE ENUMERATION OF
SHEEP, GOATS AND Pics.

[31st December, 1948.]

1. This Law may be cited as the Sheep, Goats and Pigs
(Enumeration) Law,

2. In this Law—

“enumerator '’ means any police officer or other
person appointed under section 4 (1) of this Law and
includes a rural constable when acting under section 5
of this Law.

3. For the purposes of this Law, all sheep, goats and pigs,
excluding their unweaned offspring, shall be enumerated in
every year in the manner provided in this Law, at such
period or periods as the Governor may prescribe by order
published in the Gazette and the Governor may prescribe
different periods for the enumeration of sheep, goats and
pigs, as the case may be,

4. (1) The Chief Constable of Police shall appoint as
enumerators such police officers or other persons as may be
necessary for the carrying out of the enumeration of sheep,

goats and pigs and shall prescribe the area within which
such enumerators shall be authorised to act for that purpose.

(2) Enumerators shall be appointed by the Chief
Constable of Police and the production of such appointment
or the publication of such appointment in the Gazette shall
be sufficient proof of the authority of the enumerator to act
in that behalf for the purposes of this Law.

5. Every enumerator shall have power to call upon any
rural constable operating within the town, village or area
in which the enumerator exercises authority under the
provisions of this Law, to assist him in the performance of
his duties hereunder and, thereupon, every such rural
constable shall be bound to render such assistance as may
be required in that behalf.

6. The owner of any sheep, goat or pig and any other
person in charge of any sheep, goat or pig shall render
every facility and assistance to every enumerator in carry-
ing out the enumeration under this Law and shall answer
truly all questions put to him by the enumerator in con-
nection therewith or in respect of any sheep, goat or pig
which the enumerator has any reason to believe has been
removed, taken away or concealed.

7. For the purpose of making the enumeration, it shall be
lawful for every enumerator to enter, if need be by force,
any house, building or place where he has reason to believe
that there are sheep, goats or pigs liable to be enumerated.

8. Every enumerator shall, upon completion of his task,
submit to the Chief Constable of Police a true and accurate
report in connection therewith,

9. The Chief Constable of Police shall transmit the reports
of the enumerators to the Commissioner of the district
within which the town, village or area in respect of which
the enumeration was made is situate,

10. (1) The Governor may at any time and notwith-
standing that an enumeration has been made, order a
further enumeration of any sheep, goats or pigs cither in the
whole of the Colony or in any town, village or area specified
in the order and may prescribe the period of the year during
which any such further enumeration shall take place.

(2) An order for a further enumeration shall be published
in the Gazette and, thereupon, all the provisions of this Law
shall apply to such further enumeration as they apply te
the first enumeration,

11. (1) During any period prescribed for an enumeration
under section 3 of this Law or a further enumeration under
section 10 of this Law, no sheep, goat or pig, as the case
may be, shall be moved or taken away from the town,
village or area in which such sheep, goat or pig was kept or
found at the date and time so prescribed for the commence-
ment of the enumeration or further enumeration.

(2) Any person contravening the provisions of subsection
(1) of this section shall be guilty of an offence and shall, on
summary conviction, be liable to a fine of not less than one
hundred mils nor of more than five hundred mils for every
sheep, goat or pig in respect of which the offence has been
committed and the Court trying the offence may order that
such sheep, goat or pig shall be forfeited.

12. The Chief Constable of Police may, from time to time
by writing under his hand, depute to such person as may be
specified in such writing all or any of the powers and
authorities vested in him under the provisions of this Law.

13. Any person, who wilfully obstructs or resists any
enumerator in the lawful performance of any duty under
this Law, or contravenes or fails to observe the provisions
of section 6 of this Law, shall be guilty of an offence and
shall, on summary conviction, be liable to imprisonment
for a term not exceeding three months or to a fine not
exceeding twenty-five pounds or to both.

14. Books and forms may be prepared under the direction
of the Administrative Secretary for the use of the enumera-
tors in connection with the enumeration of sheep, goats and
pigs containing such particulars as the Administrative
Secretary may direct and every enumerator shall enter in
the book or forms the details required by the particulars
in such book or form.

15. The Governor may, by order to be published in the
Gazette, suspend the operation of this Law or any provision
thereof either generally or particularly as respects sheep or
