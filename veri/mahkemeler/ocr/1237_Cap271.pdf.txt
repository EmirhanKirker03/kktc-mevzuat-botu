To AMEND THE LAW RELATING TO MINES,
[20th March, 1882.)

1. This Law may be cited as the Mines Regulations
Law.

2. In this Law the word “oil “ includes “ gas.

3. (1) Notwithstanding anything contained in this Law or
any other Law, the Governor in Council may issue to any
person making application therefor a licence to explore for
oil (hereinafter in this Law called “an oil exploration
licence ”) over such areas, for such periods, on such terms
and conditions, and on payment of such fees as to the
Governor in Council shall seem fit,

(2) Before an oi! exploration licence is issued the applicant
therefor shall produce evidence to the satisfaction of the
Governor in Council that he possesses or commands
sufficient working capital and technical knowledge or
assistance to ensure proper and adequate exploration for
oil of the area to be explored.

4. There shall be implied in every oil exploration

licence—
(a) an obligation on the part of the holder of such
licence to pay compensation to any owner or

in such licence and of surrounding land, for

holder in the exercise of the powers conferred
on him by his licence and against all costs and
expenses relating thereto.

5. Subject to the provisions of section 3 and to the terms
and conditions in his oil exploration licence contained the
holder thereof shall have the exclusive right to enter upon
any lands within the area specified in such licence for himself,
his agents or workmen, for the purpose of exploring for oil,

6. Upon the completion of the exploration of the area
specified in any oil exploration licence to the satisfaction of
the Governor in Council and in accordance with the terms and
conditions of such licence the holder thereof shall have the
right to obtain, at any time prior to the expiration of such
licence, in accordance with and subject to the provisions of
the law in force for the time being, a permit to prospect for
oil over such portion or portions of the said area as may be
selected by him.

operate so as to prevent any person otherwise entitled to
do so from prospecting or conducting mining operations
over the area specified in such licence for mineral substances

8. Notwithstanding anything contained in this Law or
any other Law, no person shall prospect for oil without a
permit by the Governor in Council. A permit to prospect
for oil may be issued to such persons, over such areas, for
such periods, and on such conditions as to the Governor in
Council shall seem fit, provided always that every such
oil permit shall contain an undertaking to make compen-
sation for damage and that security for the same shall be
taken in such amount as the Governor in Council shall
prescribe. No permit to prospect for oil shall operate to
prevent any person otherwise entitled to do so from
prospecting over the same area for mineral substances
other than oil or to prevent the Governor from issuing a
permit for such purpose, provided that such prospecting
work shall not injure the prospecting work under the
permit to prospect for oil.

9. Notwithstanding anything contained in this Law or
any other Law, the Governor in Council may grant a
licence to such persons, over such areas, for such periods,
and on such conditions as to the Governor in Council may
seem fit, to mine and win oil. Such licence shall provide a
royalty to be taken by the Government at a rate and in
manner to be provided in the said licence. Provided always
that such licence shall contain an undertaking to make
compensation for damage and that security for the same
shall be taken in such amount as the Governor in Council
shall prescribe. If the licensees cannot obtain the consent
of the landowners to sell to the licensees the land required
for the exploitation of the mine, they may, subject to the
approval of the Governor in Council, purchase the said
land upon payment to the owners of double the value
thereof, to be assessed by some person to be appointed for
the purpose by the Governor.
