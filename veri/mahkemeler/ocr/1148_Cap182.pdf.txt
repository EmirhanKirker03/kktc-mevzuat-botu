To PROVIDE FOR THE LIMITATION OF THE Hours OF
EMPLOYMENT IN CERTAIN CASES.

[16th November, 1927.]

1. This Law may be cited as the Hours of Employment
Law.

2. (1) The Governor in Council may, if he thinks fit and
after considering any recommendations of the Labour
Advisory Board in this respect, make an Order fixing the
hours of work whether daily, weekly or monthly, of persons
employed in any occupation or business, either generally
or in any specified area to which this Law shall be declared
by such Order to be applied.

(2) Any person who acts in contravention or fails to
comply with the provisions of any Order made under sub-
section (1) shall be liable to imprisonment not exceeding six
months or to a fine not exceeding one hundred pounds or to
both such imprisonment and fine.

(3) For the purposes of this section “ Labour Advisory
Board '’ means the Board established by the Governor to
advise on any labour matters and consisting of members
representing employers and employees respectively under
the chairmanship of the Commissioner of Labour.

3. An Order under this Law may make provision for the
hours of employment fixed by such Order being extended on
such occasions and for such purposes and to such extent only
as may be specified in such Order.
