To PROVIDE FOR THE SAFE CUSTODY OF PRISONERS,
IZnd May, 1888.)
1. This Law may be cited as the Convict Guards Law.

2. The Gövernor may from time to time appoint such
persons as he shall think fit to be convict guards for the
safe custody of prisoners when employed on public works
or otherwise, either within or without the walls of any
prison.

3. Every convict guard while acting as such shall by
virtue of his appointment have all such powers, authorities,
protections and privileges for the purpose of the execution
of his duty as a police constable has while acting in the
performance of his duties.

4. The Governor in Council may from time to time make
Regulations to regulate the duties to be performed by
convict guards, and for the maintenance of good order and
discipline among such guards and among the prisoners in
their custody, and prescribe punishments for breaches of
such Regulations; and all Regulations made under this
section shall have the same force as if they were enacted

in this Law.
