A LAW TO PROVIDE FOR THE BETTER PROTECTION OF
OLIVES AND OF CERTAIN OTHER CRops.

[23rd Seplember, 1938. |

1. This Law may be cited as the Olives and Other Crops
(Protection) Law.

2. (1) The Commissioner shall, with the consent of the
District Council (Mejlis Idaré) in his District, in each year
fix the date at which the gathering of olives from trees and
the collection of olives which have fallen from trees shall
begin in each village or group of two or more adjacent
villages formed into a group by notification by the Governor
under the provisions of the Rural Constables Law.

(2) Notice of the date fixed under subsection (1) shall be
posted in a conspicuous place in the village or villages
affected thereby.

(3) Subject to the provisions of section 3, any person
who—

(a) gathers olives from any tree; or
(6) collects olives which have fallen from any tree;

before the date fixed under this section shall be guilty of
an offence.

Penalty: One month's imprisonment or five pounds fine
or both.

and subject to the provisions of subsection (2) hereof, the

to be collected and the time during which they are to be
gathered or collected.

(2) The Governor may, upon the recommendation of the
District Council (Mejlis Idaré) in any District, by notice to
be published in the Gazette suspend in any year the
operation of subsection (1) as a whole or of paragraph (4)
or (b) thereof either in respect of all the villages or groups ol
villages situate within such District or of such villages or
groups of villages situate therein as may be specified in the
notice, and thereupon the provisions of subsection (1) or of
paragraph (a) or (6) thereof, as the case may be, shall cease
to apply to any such village or group of villages during the
year specified in the notice.

(3) For the purposes of this section the expression
“ registered owner ” shall include the heirs of a registered
owner.

4. No person shall—

(a) gather olives from any tree; or
(b) collect olives which have fallen from any tree;

in the interval between one hour after sunset in the evening
and one hour before sunrise in the morning except with the
previous written permission of the mukhtar of the village
within which the tree from which the olives are to be
gathered or the tree the fallen olives of which are to be
collected is situate and any person who acts in contravention
of this section shall be guilty of an offence.

Penalty: One month’s imprisonment or five pounds fine
or both.
