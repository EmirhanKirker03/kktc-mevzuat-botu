A Law to PROVIDE FOR THE CHANGE OF OFFICIAL AND
DEPARTMENTAL TITLES APPEARING IN ENACTMENTS.

[27th April, 1934. |
1. This Law may be cited as the Change of Titles Law,

2. Inthis Law,

“enactment "’ means any Law or Order of Her
Majesty in Council, and includes any Order in Council,
order, proclamation, rule, regulation, commission,
bye-law or notice made or issued under any Law or
Order of Her Majesty in Council.

3. Whenever any change,
(a) of any title of a public officer; or
(b) of the title of any Department or any branch of any
Department of the Government,

is made, the Governor in Council may by Order to be
published in the Gazette, provide that the new title shall be
substituted for the old title wherever it may appear in any
enactment and in any book or document whatsoever in use
in any Department of the Government and such new title
shall thereupon be substituted accordingly.
