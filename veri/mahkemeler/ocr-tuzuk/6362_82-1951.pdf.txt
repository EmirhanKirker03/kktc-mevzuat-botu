No. 81. THE SHOP ASSISTANTS LAWS, 1942 AND 1945.

ORDER IN COUNCIL No. 2462
MADE UNDER SECTION 19.
A. B, WRIGHT,
Governor.

In exercise of the powers vested in me by section 19 of the Shop
Assistants Laws, 1942 and 1945, I, the Governor, with the advice of the
Executive Council, do hereby order that the Second Schedule to the said
Laws be amended by the addition thereto immediately after item 14 of the
following item :—

“ıs. The sale of any goods in any premises or place on such date or
dates and at such time or times as the Commissioner may declare by notice
in the Gazette on occasions on which groups of persons visit the Colony
for a period of stay not exceeding one week.”

Ordered this 20th day of February, 1951.

D. A. SHEPHERD,
(M.P. 1354/50.) Clerk of the Executive Council.
